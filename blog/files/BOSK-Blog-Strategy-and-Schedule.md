## 1. Why this matters

Your blog page (`/blog-full-list.php`) currently shows dummy text. Google sees an empty blog as a missed opportunity — and so do customers comparing you against HomeLane, Lakdi, Interior Company and local carpenters. A consistent blog does three things for BOSK:

1. **Ranks you for buyer searches** like *"modular kitchen cost Gujarat"* and *"marine plywood vs BWR"* — questions people ask right before they buy.
2. **Builds trust** by showing off your real differentiators: IS:710 marine-grade plywood, Hettich hardware, Rehau edge bands, the 7-year warranty, 45-day delivery and 600+ completed projects.
3. **Feeds your whole funnel** — each post links to your Shop, category and Contact pages, turning readers into enquiries.

## 2. Keyword strategy

Each post targets one **primary keyword** (high buyer intent) plus a few **secondary keywords**. Mix of three content types:

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Commercial** (bottom-funnel) | Capture people ready to buy | "modular kitchen cost gujarat" |
| **Comparison** (mid-funnel) | Win the research stage | "custom vs readymade furniture" |
| **Educational** (top-funnel) | Build authority + authentic differentiation | "marine plywood IS 710 explained" |

**Local angle:** Always include *Gujarat / Bhavnagar / Ahmedabad / Rajkot / Saurashtra* where natural. You already serve Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Amreli, Jamnagar, Junagadh and Vadodara (per your testimonials) — local keywords are far easier to rank for than national ones.

---

## 3. On-page SEO checklist (apply to EVERY post)

- [ ] **SEO title** ≤ 60 characters, primary keyword near the front
- [ ] **Meta description** 150–160 characters, includes keyword + a reason to click
- [ ] **URL slug** short, hyphenated, keyword-only (e.g. `/modular-kitchen-cost-gujarat`)
- [ ] **One H1** (the post title) — never repeat H1 on the page
- [ ] Primary keyword in the **first 100 words**, in **one H2**, and in the **conclusion**
- [ ] **Internal links** to 2–3 of: Shop, relevant category, Warranty, How It Works, Contact
- [ ] **Image** with descriptive filename + **alt text** containing the keyword
- [ ] **FAQ block** at the end (helps you win Google's "People Also Ask" boxes)
- [ ] **One clear CTA** — phone (+91 88666 47777 / +91 97378 17777) or the Contact page
- [ ] Word count **1,200–1,600** for full posts
- [ ] Add **FAQ schema (JSON-LD)** if your developer (Two12) can — boosts rich results

> **Pro tip:** Real photos beat AI images for trust and SEO. Use your own project photos from the deck (Regenta, Trident, TATA Solar, your kitchens & wardrobes) wherever possible. The AI image prompts in each post are a fallback for when you don't have a suitable photo.

## 6. How to publish each post (quick workflow)

1. Copy the blog body (everything under "BODY") into your CMS blog editor.
2. Set the **SEO title**, **meta description** and **slug** from the post's header block.
3. Add the image — use your own project photo if you have one; otherwise generate one from the prompt provided.
4. Paste the **alt text** on the image.
5. Add the internal links listed.
6. (Optional) Ask Two12 to add the **FAQ JSON-LD** schema.
7. Publish on the scheduled date, then share to Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp Status with the first line as the caption.
